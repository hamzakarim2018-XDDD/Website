1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how AgoraCrew (hereinafter referred to as "we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, stores, and protects personal data in connection with our custom software engineering services, our Shopify applications, our HubSpot and monday.com marketplace applications, enterprise integration platforms, and our corporate website at agoracrew.com.

This policy applies to:

  • Clients and prospects who engage with us for custom software development, enterprise integrations, or AI automation projects.
  • End customers whose data may be processed when a merchant installs and uses one of our Shopify applications (e.g., Order Exceptions Copilot).
  • Website visitors who interact with our website, contact forms, or AI-powered chat widget.
  • Users of our AI Support Agent & Growth Engine whose interactions are processed to deliver customer support and revenue recovery services on behalf of e-commerce merchants.

We are committed to full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the UK Data Protection Act 2018, and Shopify's App Store Developer Guidelines.

🔒 ARBridge for QuickBooks — architecture note: ARBridge is a HubSpot Marketplace app. For each customer who installs it, it reads Customer, Invoice, and Payment records from that customer's own QuickBooks Desktop company file via QuickBooks Web Connector (using the qbXML protocol) and writes a derived financial picture into that same customer's own HubSpot account. Data is never aggregated or shared across customers, and is never sold or used for any purpose beyond this sync. ARBridge only ever issues read-only qbXML "Query" requests — there is no code path in the product capable of adding, modifying, or deleting anything in a customer's QuickBooks file; this is not a configuration setting, the write capability does not exist in the code. ARBridge stores two categories of credentials, both encrypted at rest (AES-256): a QuickBooks Web Connector username/password pair generated by ARBridge for that one connection, and a HubSpot OAuth refresh token, which is rotated. ARBridge never sees or stores a customer's actual HubSpot login credentials — access is via OAuth only. All data in transit uses TLS.

🔒 XSync for QuickBooks Desktop — architecture note: XSync for QuickBooks Desktop is a monday.com marketplace app. For each customer who installs it, it reads Customer, Job, Invoice, Estimate, Sales Order and Item records from that customer's own QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise company file via QuickBooks Web Connector (using the qbXML protocol) and creates and updates items on that same customer's own monday.com boards, according to a field mapping that customer configures. Data is never aggregated or shared across customers, and is never sold or used for any purpose beyond this sync. Like its HubSpot counterpart, it only ever issues read-only qbXML "Query" requests — there is no code path in the product capable of adding, modifying, or deleting anything in a customer's QuickBooks file; this is not a configuration setting, the write capability does not exist in the code. Credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM under a versioned key ring: a QuickBooks Web Connector username/password pair generated for that one connection, and a monday.com OAuth refresh token, which is rotated on every use. The app never sees or stores a customer's actual monday.com login credentials — access is via OAuth only. All data in transit uses TLS. One consequence worth stating plainly: QuickBooks sends whole record types, so records of a type a customer has not enabled or whose plan does not yet include it are still read and held in staging, encrypted, rather than being discarded. They are not sent anywhere. This is what allows an upgrade or an un-pause to take effect on the next sync instead of requiring a fresh read of the customer's entire history, and that staged data is erased on the same ten-day clock as everything else.

4. Data Minimization

We adhere strictly to the GDPR principle of data minimization (Article 5(1)(c)). This means:

  • We only process data that is strictly necessary to deliver the specific service — whether that's a custom software project, a Shopify app integration, or an AI-powered support interaction.
  • No additional data is requested, scraped, inferred, or collected beyond what is explicitly required for the stated purpose.
  • We do not build customer profiles, track browsing behaviour, or aggregate personal data for marketing purposes.

5. AI & Automated Processing Disclosure

In accordance with transparency requirements under GDPR (Articles 13–14) and Shopify's App Store guidelines, we inform all users of the following:

AI Interaction Notice: Several of our products and services use AI-powered automation. This includes our AI Support Agent & Growth Engine (which handles customer support conversations), our Order Exceptions Copilot (which analyses order data for operational issues), and AI components within custom software we build for clients. These AI systems process data to generate responses, identify patterns, and automate workflows.

Our AI systems operate under the following principles:

  • No autonomous decision-making: Our AI does not make decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect data subjects. Its function is to draft support replies, identify operational exceptions, and automate predefined workflows.
  • Human oversight: All AI-powered systems include escalation mechanisms so that complex or sensitive matters can be reviewed and handled by a human.
  • Brand-aligned responses: AI support agents are configured to match the merchant's specific brand voice, tone, and support policies. They do not generate unsolicited content.
  • No model training on your data: Your data is never used to train, fine-tune, or improve third-party AI models. We maintain contractual agreements that explicitly prohibit this.
  • Right to human intervention: Under Article 22 of the GDPR, you have the right to request human intervention at any stage. Contact the merchant directly or reach out to us at the email address provided in this policy.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We apply strict data retention practices:

  • Client project data: Retained for the duration of the active engagement plus 12 months for support and warranty purposes. Deleted upon request after that period.
  • AI support interaction data: Customer data processed during a support interaction is deleted from our active caches immediately following ticket resolution. We do not maintain long-term databases of end-customer personal data.
  • Shopify app data: When a merchant uninstalls one of our apps, all associated customer data is purged from our systems within 48 hours of receiving the uninstallation webhook.
  • Contact form submissions: Retained for up to 24 months for business development follow-up, then deleted.
  • Erasure requests: We honour all data erasure requests in full compliance with GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure). All requests are processed within 30 calendar days of receipt.
  • Anonymised logs: We may retain fully anonymised and aggregated usage logs (containing no personal data) for service improvement and performance monitoring.
  • ARBridge for QuickBooks — synced HubSpot data: Data ARBridge has synced into a customer's own HubSpot account (Companies, Contacts, Invoice records, and derived AR fields) is not deleted or retracted by ARBridge when the customer disconnects or uninstalls the app. It remains ordinary CRM data under that customer's own control, since it resides in an account they own.
  • ARBridge for QuickBooks — connection credentials & sync logs: As of the date of this policy, ARBridge's backend does not yet automatically purge a disconnected connection's stored encrypted credentials (QuickBooks Web Connector password, HubSpot OAuth refresh token) or historical sync logs. This is a known gap that we are tracking for a future fix; customers who want this data deleted manually can contact us at the email address in Section 13.
  • ARBridge for QuickBooks — GDPR erasure requests: When HubSpot notifies ARBridge of a contact.privacyDeletion event (an end-customer of our customer exercising their GDPR right to be forgotten), ARBridge permanently purges that contact's personal data (email address and name) from its own database and durably prevents QuickBooks from re-populating that same data on a future sync.
  • XSync for QuickBooks Desktop — synced monday.com data: Items the app has created on a customer's own monday.com boards are not deleted or retracted when that customer disconnects or uninstalls it. They remain ordinary board data under the customer's own control, since they reside in an account they own.
  • XSync for QuickBooks Desktop — staged QuickBooks data, credentials & sync logs: When monday.com tells us a customer has uninstalled the app or revoked its authorisation, a deletion clock starts. Unless the app is reconnected, everything held for that connection — the staged QuickBooks records, the encrypted QuickBooks Web Connector credentials, the monday.com tokens, the board mappings and the sync history — is permanently erased within ten days, and in practice within seven. Deletion is deferred rather than immediate because uninstalling and reinstalling is a normal way to troubleshoot a problem, and an instant purge would destroy a customer's entire setup during their own attempt to fix it. Reconnecting stops the clock.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure — Request deletion of your personal data ("Right to Be Forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction of Processing — Request limitation of processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object — Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Human Intervention — Request that automated decisions (including AI-generated responses) be reviewed by a human.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at karim@agoracrew.com. We will respond to all valid requests within 30 calendar days. If your request is complex, we will inform you of any extension (up to a maximum of 90 days total) as permitted under GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. For Hungary, this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH).

10. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • TLS 1.2+ encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest. In our QuickBooks integrations this is applied at the column level as well as by the hosting provider: personal data — customer and contact names, email addresses, billing addresses, and free-text notes — is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a versioned key ring before it is written, so it is not readable from the database itself
  • Access controls based on the principle of least privilege
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Mandatory data protection training for all personnel with data access
  • Secure API authentication using OAuth 2.0 for platform integrations (Shopify, HubSpot, QuickBooks, monday.com)
  • Encrypted credential storage for all integration tokens and API keys
