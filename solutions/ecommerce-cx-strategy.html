The Hidden Costs Most Founders Miss

  • Training time — every new agent needs 2–4 weeks to learn your products, policies, and brand voice
  • Turnover — average support agent tenure is 12–18 months. You'll retrain frequently
  • Tool costs — helpdesk software ($50–$300/mo), phone systems, QA tools
  • Management overhead — someone has to review quality, update scripts, handle escalations
  • Opportunity cost — every hour you spend in the inbox is an hour not spent on growth
The playbook

Building Your CX Playbook

A CX playbook is a living document that defines how your brand handles every type of customer interaction. It's the difference between consistent, on-brand support and ad-hoc guessing.

Define your response time targets

Set explicit targets for each channel. Example: email within 4 hours, chat within 60 seconds, social DMs within 2 hours. Post these targets publicly — it builds accountability and trust.

Write Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Document how to handle every common scenario: WISMO, returns under $50, returns over $50, damaged items, lost packages, subscription cancellations. Include exact response templates and decision trees.

Build an escalation framework

Not every ticket can be resolved at first contact. Define when an agent (or AI) should escalate: customer is upset, order value exceeds $X, legal/compliance concerns, VIP customer flag.

Define your brand voice

Is your brand casual or professional? Do you use emojis? First names? A consistent voice builds trust. Write 5–10 "voice guidelines" and include good/bad examples for each.

Create a QA checklist

Review 10–20 tickets per week. Score each on: accuracy, tone, resolution speed, and whether the SOP was followed. This is how you ensure quality at scale.

Measurement

Measuring What Matters

Don't drown in dashboards. Focus on these four metrics — they tell you everything you need to know about your CX health:

First Response Time (FRT)

How fast do you reply? Below 1 hour is good. Below 5 minutes (with AI) is great. This is the single biggest predictor of customer satisfaction.

Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)

Ask after every interaction. Aim for 90%+. If you're below 85%, your process has a problem — not just individual agents.

First Contact Resolution (FCR)

Was the issue resolved without a follow-up? 80%+ is the target. Low FCR means your agents lack information or authority to resolve issues.

Cost Per Ticket

Total support spend ÷ tickets resolved. Track this monthly. If it's rising, you're either overstaffed or under-automated.

Scaling

Scaling Your Strategy as You Grow

Your CX strategy at 100 orders/month should look different from your strategy at 10,000. Here's a rough guide:

Stage Monthly Orders Recommended Setup
Bootstrapping 0–200 Founder handles support + self-service FAQ + basic chat widget
Growth 200–1,000 AI agent for WISMO + repetitive tickets. Founder handles escalations only
Scaling 1,000–5,000 AI-first with dedicated human agents for complex issues. Full helpdesk integration
Enterprise 5,000+ Multi-channel AI + nearshore/offshore human team. Dedicated account manager. Custom integrations
The golden rule of scaling

Automate the repetitive before you hire the expensive. Every ticket you automate today is a ticket you never have to train a human to handle.
