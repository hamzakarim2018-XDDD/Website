What We Automate

The Work Nobody
Hired Your Team To Do.

If someone on your team opens one system, reads a number and types it into another, a robot can usually do it. These are the processes we get asked about most.

Finance operations

AP, AR, treasury and the close

Accounts payable

Invoices arrive by email, get read, checked for duplicates, matched to the PO and goods receipt, and posted to your ERP with the PDF attached. Mismatches go to a person with the reason already written down.

Cash application & collections

Bank receipts matched to open invoices, including the payments that cover eight invoices and the ones short by a credit note. Reminders go out on schedule, in your team’s words, and stop the day the customer pays.

Reconciliations & month-end

Bank, card and intercompany reconciliations run every day instead of on day three of the close. Recurring journals, accruals and the reporting pack are prepared before anyone logs in.

Vendor & customer master data

New supplier and customer forms checked, bank details compared against the documents, and records created in the ERP with a full audit trail. Fewer duplicates, and fewer chances for invoice fraud.

Client service

The requests that fill your shared inbox

Inbox triage

Every email sorted by request type, customer and urgency. Invoice copies, statements and order status questions answered with live data from your ERP. Disputes and complaints routed to the right person.

Order entry

Purchase orders that arrive as PDFs or emails keyed into the ERP, checked against price lists and stock, and confirmed back to the customer.

Credits, refunds & disputes

The request is logged, the invoice and payment history pulled together, and a ready-to-approve case put in front of whoever signs it off. No more digging through three systems per ticket.

CRM and ERP in sync

Balances, open invoices and credit status on the customer record in your CRM, so account managers stop emailing finance to ask what a customer owes.

FAQ

Questions Finance Teams
Ask Us First.

Do we need to change our ERP or other systems?

No. We automate around what you already have. Robots work through the same screens and files your team uses, and through the ERP’s API where it has a good one.

What’s the difference between RPA and AI here?

RPA (robotic process automation) is software that clicks, types and copies between systems the way a person does, following fixed rules. AI handles the parts that aren’t fixed: reading an invoice layout it hasn’t seen before, or working out what a customer’s email is asking for. Most processes need both. More on that here.

Is our financial data safe?

Each automation runs under its own user account with only the access it needs, and every action is logged. Nothing posts to the ledger or moves money outside the rules and thresholds you approve.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the process and how many systems it touches. You get a timeline with the fixed quote, before you commit to anything.

What does it cost?

The process audit is free. Each build is quoted at a fixed price once we’ve mapped the process, and ongoing monitoring and support is a monthly fee quoted at the same time. No open-ended hourly billing.

Which processes pay back fastest?

High volume, rule-based and painful: invoice entry, cash application, bank reconciliations, order entry and shared-inbox requests. Start with one, prove it on your own numbers, then do the next. Here’s how we score them.

Will this replace our finance team?

It replaces the keying and the chasing. Your team keeps the work that needs judgment: exceptions, approvals, supplier and customer relationships, and the close. Most teams we talk to want to stop hiring for volume, not to let people go.
