Why this is specialist work

QuickBooks Desktop Has
No API You Can Call.

Cloud accounting has a REST API and an OAuth button. Desktop and Enterprise have neither. They sit on a machine in your office, behind your firewall, and they only talk when they decide to. That single fact is why most integrations quietly skip them — and why we built for them on purpose.

↩

QuickBooks calls you, not the reverse

The only supported route in is the QuickBooks Web Connector — a small Windows program that polls a SOAP service on a schedule and pushes qbXML at it. You cannot request data on demand; you answer when it knocks. Every design decision downstream follows from that constraint.

☁

Almost every rival connects to QuickBooks Online

Search either marketplace and the QuickBooks integrations you find target the cloud product. A firm running Enterprise on-premises — most construction, manufacturing and wholesale companies we speak to — is left doing CSV exports by hand. That is the gap both our apps sit in.

🔒

Read-only, and we mean it

Data moves one way: out of QuickBooks and into your CRM. Neither app writes, edits or deletes anything in your company file. Your bookkeeper’s ledger stays exactly as they left it, and the worst case for a sync bug is a stale figure in your CRM — never a corrupted book.

⚖

The accounting has to actually be right

A balance is not just the sum of invoices. Credit memos, partial payments, discounts, write-offs and multi-currency all move the number, and qbXML will happily return a valid-looking response that quietly omits the field you needed. Getting these right is most of the work, and it is the part you cannot see from a screenshot.

How It Works

From Call to
Live Data.

The apps install themselves. Connecting QuickBooks Desktop is the part that needs a human, so we do it with you.

01

Book a call

Twenty minutes. We look at your QuickBooks version, how your company file is hosted and which CRM you run, and tell you plainly whether this fits. If it doesn’t, we say so on the call.

02

Install and connect

You install the app from HubSpot or monday. We set up the QuickBooks Web Connector on the machine that runs QuickBooks and authorise it against your company file — screen-share, with you driving your own machine.

03

History backfills, then it keeps up

The first sync pulls your existing customers and invoices across. After that the Web Connector runs on a schedule and only moves what changed, so your CRM stays current without anyone touching it.

⚠

One thing we won’t pretend about

This is not a two-click OAuth integration, because QuickBooks Desktop does not offer one. The Web Connector is a Windows program that has to be installed and authorised on the machine holding your company file, and that machine has to be on for syncs to run. Setup is genuinely hands-on — which is exactly why the onboarding call is part of the product rather than an upsell.
